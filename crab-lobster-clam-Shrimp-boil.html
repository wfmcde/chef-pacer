Dungeness Crab, Lobster Tails, Shrimp & Manila Clam Boil

15.5-qt Granite Ware single-pot boil with 2 Maine lobster tails, 16/20 peeled & deveined Gulf shrimp, baby red potatoes, fennel wedges, sweet corn, mushrooms, and garlic lemon butter.

Ingredients

  • 2 Jumbo whole cooked Dungeness crabs
  • 2 raw Maine lobster tails (split down top shell if desired)
  • 1 lb live Manila clams
  • 0.5 lb 16/20 peeled & deveined Gulf shrimp (thawed/rinsed)
  • 1 lb baby red potatoes & 1 lb fennel bulb (6 wedges)
  • 3 ears supersweet corn (halved) & 0.5 lb whole cremini mushrooms
  • 3.5–4 qts water, 1 cup dry white wine, 3–4 tbsp Old Bay
  • 1 yellow onion, 1 bulb garlic, 2 lemons, Italian parsley
  • 1 stick butter & crusty baguette

Hardware Setup

  • 15.5-qt Granite Ware pot + lid + perforated insert (domed side up)
  • Long kitchen tongs & slotted spoon / spider strainer
  • Granite Ware mallets, crackers, shears & picks
  • Large serving platter, broth dipping bowls & small butter saucepan
