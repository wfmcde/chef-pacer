Thick Pork Chops & Mushroom Ravioli

Gotham Greens Vegan Pesto, upright fat-rendering, and single-threaded rest-and-boil execution.

Meal Itinerary Cadence Leg 1 of 5
Mise en Place & Equipment Checklist

Ingredients

  • 2 bone-in pork chops (1.75" thick, fat strip intact)
  • 1 package mushroom ravioli
  • Gotham Greens Vegan Pesto
  • Kosher salt, black pepper, garlic powder
  • Reserved hot pasta cooking water (1/2 cup)

Hardware Setup

  • Outdoor grill with 2-zone heat setup (400°F)
  • Large pot of salted water on stove
  • Tongs & aluminum foil
  • Coffee mug or ladle (for hot pasta water)
Phase 1: Pre-Heat & Prep
Grill Setup (400°F)

Sensory Exit Criteria

The Thumb Pad Benchmark: Touch your ring finger tip to your thumb tip. Press the fleshy mound at the base of your thumb with your other hand. That springy resistance is what 140°F–145°F pork feels like. If it feels squishy like raw meat, keep roasting.
