The Thumb Pad Benchmark: Touch the tip of your ring finger to your thumb tip. Press the fleshy mound at the base of your thumb. That springy, pliable pushback is what a medium (140°F–145°F) pork chop feels like. If it feels like your open palm (squishy), it needs more time.
