Pan-Seared Shrimp, Scallops & Heirloom Tomatoes

Caramelized cremini mushrooms, juicy burst heirloom tomatoes, and pasta tossed with pesto.

Meal Itinerary Cadence Leg 1 of 5
Mise en Place & Equipment Checklist

Ingredients

  • 3/4 lb peeled shrimp & bay scallops (patted dry)
  • 8 oz pasta (linguine, fettuccine, or farfalle)
  • 2 ripe heirloom tomatoes (diced) or sweet cherry tomatoes
  • 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, olive oil, splash of dry white wine
  • Basil pesto & reserved starchy pasta water

Hardware Setup

  • Large stainless steel or cast-iron skillet
  • Large pasta pot with salted boiling water
  • Fish spatula or tongs & clean holding plate
  • Coffee mug (for reserved pasta water)
